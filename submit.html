Essential information

Before you submit

  • Open to filmmakers worldwide, aged 10 and above.
  • All genres, lengths, and formats are eligible.
  • Submission information must be provided in English.
  • Non-Korean films require Korean subtitles for screening.
  • Official selections are announced through FilmFreeway.
  • Screening details follow after the venue is confirmed.
