Awards

The AKFF Awards recognize bold visions, distinct voices, and cinematic integrity across diverse forms of filmmaking.

Awards & jury

Films selected for competition will be evaluated by an international jury appointed by Ara Korea Film Festival. Winners will be officially announced during the festival period.

  • Awards and Certificates for the Winner in Each Category
  • Official Selection Laurels for Selected Films
  • Selected filmmakers will be notified by email in advance of the public announcement.

Please note: If, at the discretion of the jury, no film meets the festival’s artistic standards in a category, awards may not be presented for that category.

Rules & Terms

AKFF is committed to providing a fair, transparent, and professional environment for filmmakers from around the world. By submitting a film to AKFF, applicants agree to the following rules and terms.

1. Eligibility

  • Submissions are open to filmmakers aged 10 years and above.
  • Films may be submitted by individuals, groups, organizations, institutions, clubs, or small businesses.
  • Submissions are accepted from all countries.
  • All genres and formats are eligible unless otherwise specified in category descriptions.

2. Language requirements

  • Films in languages other than Korean must include Korean subtitles.
  • Subtitles must be clear, accurate, and properly synchronized.
  • Films submitted without Korean subtitles may be disqualified from consideration.
  • Subtitles must comply with FilmFreeway’s technical guidelines.

3. Submission guidelines

  • All submissions must be made through FilmFreeway.
  • Each film must be submitted with accurate and complete information.
  • The submitting party confirms that they hold all necessary rights for exhibition, screening, and promotion of the submitted work.
  • AKFF is not responsible for any copyright infringement related to submitted materials.

4. Selection process

  • Films are selected at the discretion of the AKFF programming committee.
  • Selection decisions are final and not subject to appeal.
  • Selected filmmakers will be notified by email prior to public announcements.

5. Awards & jury

  • Films in competition will be evaluated by a jury appointed by AKFF.
  • Awards and certificates will be presented to winners in each competitive category.
  • If, in the judgment of the jury, no film meets the festival’s standards in a category, no award may be presented in that category.
  • Jury decisions are final.

6. Screening & promotion rights

  • By submitting a film, the filmmaker grants AKFF the right to screen the film as part of the festival program.
  • AKFF may use excerpts, trailers, still images, and promotional materials for marketing, publicity, and archival purposes, with proper credit.
  • No full film will be used for promotional purposes without prior consent.

7. Fees & refund policy

  • Submission fees, if applicable, are non-refundable.
  • AKFF does not provide fee waivers unless explicitly stated.

8. General terms

  • AKFF reserves the right to make changes to the festival program, schedule, and venues if necessary.
  • AKFF reserves the right to disqualify any submission that does not comply with these rules.
  • By submitting, filmmakers acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to all rules and terms outlined above.

9. Governing language

  • In the event of any dispute or discrepancy, the English version of these rules shall prevail.
