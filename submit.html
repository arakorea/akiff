Ara Korea Film Festival

Submission

Ara Korea Film Festival (AKFF) accepts film submissions exclusively through FilmFreeway.

Submit via FilmFreeway

General information

  • AKFF is an offline, in-person film festival held in Korea.
  • All films are reviewed by the festival programming team.
  • Selected films will be screened at the official festival venue.

Eligibility

  • Open to filmmakers worldwide (ages 10+).
  • All genres and formats are welcome.
  • Submission information must be provided in English (official language).

Selection & screening

  • Official selections will be announced on FilmFreeway.
  • Selected filmmakers will be contacted directly by the festival.
  • Non-Korean films must include Korean subtitles for screening.
  • Screening details will be provided after venue confirmation.
