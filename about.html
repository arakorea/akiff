Essential answers

AKFF, clearly explained.

What is Ara Korea Film Festival?

Ara Korea Film Festival, or AKFF, is an international in-person film festival held in Korea for independent, emerging, and experimental filmmakers worldwide.

Who can submit a film?

Filmmakers aged 10 and above may submit from any country. Individuals, groups, organizations, institutions, clubs, and small businesses are eligible.

What kinds of films does AKFF accept?

AKFF accepts every genre, length, and format. Feature, short, documentary, dance, experimental, and new-media works are considered together.

How are films submitted?

All entries are submitted exclusively through the official AKFF page on FilmFreeway. Submission information must be provided in English.

Is AKFF an online festival?

No. AKFF is an offline, in-person festival in Korea. The confirmed venue and screening schedule will be announced separately.

How many awards does AKFF present?

AKFF presents four awards in total: Best Film, Best Director, Best Performance, and the Jury Special Prize.
